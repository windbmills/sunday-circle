# Sunday Circle

Ward bulletin, talk video, lesson, and signed-in thoughts.

## Who signs in

- Secretary — cover, lesson, talk video link, add people, QR
- Teacher — sign in, watch the talk, post a thought before class
- Student — sign in through the week, watch the talk, post a thought

## Secretary each week

1. Sign in as secretary.
2. Cover tab — bulletin. Publish.
3. Lesson tab — title, scripture, note, **talk video link**, questions. Publish.
4. People tab — add each teacher and student once.
5. QR & link — share with the class.

YouTube and Vimeo play on the page. Gospel Library or Drive links become “Open the talk.”
